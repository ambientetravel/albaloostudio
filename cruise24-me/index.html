Dining

Nine restaurants, or three, or one very good one

Main dining is always in the fare. Speciality restaurants are extra on most lines and worth it about half the time. We tell you which half, for your ship.

Wellness

Sea days are for the thermal suite

Thermal passes sell out before the ship does. If it matters to you, we book it at the same time as the cabin.

What the fare includes

Depends on the line. Here is the honest version.

On Explora Journeys almost everything is in the fare, which is why the number looks high. On MSC, Celestyal and AROYA the fare is the start and drinks, Wi‑Fi and the service charge come on top. We quote the all-in figure for whichever ship you choose.
