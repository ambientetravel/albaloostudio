Greek island cruises from Athens

55 itineraries in our inventory, each linked below to its own page with ports, ship and fare.

The route

Round trips from Athens start and end at Piraeus, or at one of the smaller harbours near the city such as Lavrio. Between those two days they sail the Cyclades, the Dodecanese or the Saronic and Ionian islands, depending on the itinerary.

Small ships can use island harbours the big ones anchor off, and a week is enough for five or six islands. Every Greek port is in Schengen, so these are Schengen cruises; the islands and visa line for each departure are below.

Visa on this route

The rule follows the ports, not where you board: one call in a Schengen country puts the whole cruise under Schengen rules. Your residence visa does not change this; your passport does.

  • Schengen: Schengen rules for the whole cruise: a Schengen visa, unless your passport is Schengen visa-exempt. Türkiye: visa-free or e-visa for many passports, not all. We confirm the visa position for your passport before you pay anything. (36 of 55 itineraries)
