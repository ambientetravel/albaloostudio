Visa guide

Do I need a Schengen visa for a Mediterranean cruise?

Almost always the Schengen rules apply. Of the 655 Mediterranean itineraries in our inventory today, 651 call at a Schengen port.

The rule

A cruise is judged by the ports it calls at, not by where you board. If even one port is in a Schengen country, such as Greece, Italy, Spain, France, Malta or Croatia, the Schengen visa rules apply to the whole voyage. You need a Schengen visa for it unless your passport is Schengen visa-exempt.

Boarding outside Schengen does not change that. A cruise from Istanbul that calls at a single Greek island is a Schengen cruise. Of the itineraries in our inventory that start in Istanbul, 18 call at a Schengen port.

Where you live does not change it either. A UAE or other GCC residence visa is not a Schengen visa; what decides is your passport.
