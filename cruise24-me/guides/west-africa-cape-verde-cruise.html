Cruises to West Africa and the Cape Verde islands

21 itineraries in our inventory, each linked below to its own page with ports, ship and fare.

The route

Cape Verde is an archipelago of ten volcanic islands in the Atlantic, off the coast of Senegal. Ocean ships call on the way between Europe, the Caribbean and South America. Small expedition ships sail island-to-island itineraries of their own.

Further south and east the inventory includes the Gambia River, sailed inland from Banjul, and the coast of West Africa from Dakar to the Gulf of Guinea. Visa rules here vary country by country, and several of these routes also call in the Canary Islands, Madeira or Portugal, which brings in Schengen rules. Each sailing below carries its own visa line.

In our inventory today: 21 itineraries with 30 departures, 7 to 25 nights, departing from November 2026 to April 2028.

Visa on this route

The rule follows the ports, not where you board: one call in a Schengen country puts the whole cruise under Schengen rules. Your residence visa does not change this; your passport does.

  • Schengen: Schengen rules for the whole cruise: a Schengen visa, unless your passport is Schengen visa-exempt. We confirm the visa position for your passport before you pay anything. (13 of 21 itineraries)
