Lower Danube river cruises: Budapest to Bucharest

31 itineraries in our inventory, each linked below to its own page with ports, ship and fare.

The route

Below Budapest the Danube turns south through Hungary, Croatia and Serbia, and passes Belgrade. It then forms the border between Serbia and Romania through the Iron Gates gorge, and the border between Bulgaria and Romania on its way to the Black Sea.

Bucharest is not on the river. These itineraries end (or begin) with the road between the Danube and the city, and the inventory lists the last river ports before it. Fewer ships sail this stretch than the upper Danube, so departures are fewer; every one in the inventory is listed below.

In our inventory today: 31 itineraries with 31 departures, 8 to 19 nights, departing from March 2027 to April 2028.

Visa on this route

The rule follows the ports, not where you board: one call in a Schengen country puts the whole cruise under Schengen rules. Your residence visa does not change this; your passport does.

  • Schengen: Schengen rules for the whole cruise: a Schengen visa, unless your passport is Schengen visa-exempt. Serbia is outside Schengen: if you need a Schengen visa, it should be multiple-entry. We confirm the visa position for your passport before you pay anything. (31 of 31 itineraries)
