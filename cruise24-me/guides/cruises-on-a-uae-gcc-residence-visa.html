Which cruises can I take on a UAE or GCC residence visa?

Your residence visa lets you live in the Gulf and come home to it. Which cruises you can take depends on your passport and the ports a ship calls at.

The short answer

A UAE, Saudi, Qatari, Omani, Kuwaiti or Bahraini residence permit is not itself a visa for the countries a cruise visits. A few countries ease their rules for Gulf residents, and we check that for you. For a cruise, two things decide what you need. The first is your passport. The second is the ports: one Schengen port puts the whole voyage under Schengen rules, and one US port under US rules.
