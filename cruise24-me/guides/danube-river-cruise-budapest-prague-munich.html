Upper Danube river cruises: Budapest, Vienna, Prague and Munich

61 itineraries in our inventory, each linked below to its own page with ports, ship and fare.

The route

The upper Danube links Budapest, Bratislava, Vienna, the Wachau valley and Passau, on the border between Austria and Germany. Neither Prague nor Munich is on the river. Itineraries that start or end in one of them spend the first or last days in that city on land, and join the ship on the Danube.

It is the shortest of the classic central European river routes, and the one with the most capital cities per day of sailing.

In our inventory today: 61 itineraries with 81 departures, 7 to 13 nights, departing from November 2026 to April 2028.

Who sails it

Ports called at most often

Bratislava, Vienna, Passau, Budapest, Regensburg, Prague, Melk-Duernstein and Durnstein-Melk.

Visa on this route

The rule follows the ports, not where you board: one call in a Schengen country puts the whole cruise under Schengen rules. Your residence visa does not change this; your passport does.

  • Schengen: Schengen rules for the whole cruise: a Schengen visa, unless your passport is Schengen visa-exempt. We confirm the visa position for your passport before you pay anything. (61 of 61 itineraries)
