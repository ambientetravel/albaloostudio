Cruises between Istanbul and Athens

20 itineraries in our inventory, each linked below to its own page with ports, ship and fare.

The route

Istanbul and Athens are joined by the Aegean: out of the Bosphorus and the Sea of Marmara, past the Dardanelles, and down the Turkish coast and the Greek islands to Piraeus, the port of Athens.

Starting in Istanbul does not keep a cruise out of Schengen. Athens and every Greek island are in the Schengen area, so the Schengen visa rule applies to the whole voyage the moment one Greek port is on it. Each departure below carries its visa line.

Visa on this route

The rule follows the ports, not where you board: one call in a Schengen country puts the whole cruise under Schengen rules. Your residence visa does not change this; your passport does.

  • Schengen: Schengen rules for the whole cruise: a Schengen visa, unless your passport is Schengen visa-exempt. Türkiye: visa-free or e-visa for many passports, not all. We confirm the visa position for your passport before you pay anything. (16 of 20 itineraries)
