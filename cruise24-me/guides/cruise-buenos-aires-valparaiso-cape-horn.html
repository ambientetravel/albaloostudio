Cruises between Buenos Aires and Valparaíso around Cape Horn

11 itineraries in our inventory, each linked below to its own page with ports, ship and fare.

The route

Valparaíso is Chile's port for Santiago, and Buenos Aires is Argentina's capital on the Río de la Plata. Between them a ship goes round the bottom of South America. It sails the Chilean fjords, through or around the Strait of Magellan and past Cape Horn, and calls at Ushuaia, the Falkland Islands and the Uruguayan coast.

Some voyages in the inventory continue to South Georgia and the Antarctic Peninsula before returning north. Those details are in each sailing's port list.

In our inventory today: 11 itineraries with 12 departures, 12 to 67 nights, departing from November 2026 to March 2028.

Visa on this route

The rule follows the ports, not where you board: one call in a Schengen country puts the whole cruise under Schengen rules. Your residence visa does not change this; your passport does.

  • Confirmed for your passport: We confirm the visa position for your passport before you pay anything. (9 of 11 itineraries)
  • Schengen: Schengen rules for the whole cruise: a Schengen visa, unless your passport is Schengen visa-exempt. Türkiye: visa-free or e-visa for many passports, not all. We confirm the visa position for your passport before you pay anything. (1 of 11 itineraries)
  • Türkiye: Türkiye: visa-free or e-visa for many passports, not all. We confirm the visa position for your passport before you pay anything. (1 of 11 itineraries)
