About us

The world of cruising, from people you can name

Cruise24 brings the world's cruise lines to travellers in the Middle East. Behind it is Ambiente Group: a small family of travel companies that plans journeys, congresses and voyages the same way, with the real price and the visa position on the table before anyone pays.

Cruise24

What we do

We book ocean cruises, river weeks and luxury small-ship journeys, with the lines we know best: Explora Journeys, Silversea, Variety Cruises and Scenic first, and the lines that sail the same waters. We start where most of our guests want to go: Türkiye, Greece and Italy.

Every quote tells you the cabin price the line will really charge, the service charge, and what your passport needs for every port on the route. One planner stays on your file from the first question to the gangway.

For companies, we plan meetings, incentives and celebrations on board, from a board retreat to a full-ship charter: MICE at sea.
