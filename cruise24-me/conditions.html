Booking conditions · Reisebedingungen

How a booking works, in writing

These conditions apply to every booking made through AmbiMare, the sea collection of Ambiente Tours GmbH. Where AmbiMare arranges a cruise on behalf of a cruise line, the line's own terms apply to the cruise itself and are handed to you before you commit. The German version prevails where the law requires it.

1. Who you are contracting with · Vertragspartner

  • Arranged booking (Reisevermittlung). AmbiMare arranges the cruise on your behalf with the cruise line. The package travel contract is between you and the line, which is the organiser (Reiseveranstalter) under §§ 651a ff. BGB; its booking conditions govern the cruise, its cancellation scale applies, and its insolvency protection covers your payments. AmbiMare's own obligations are those of an agent under §§ 651v BGB: correct advice, correct transmission of the booking, and the pre-contractual information in Art. 250 EGBGB.
  • Own package (Reiseveranstaltung). Where AmbiMare combines a cruise with flights, hotels or transfers into one package, or otherwise acts as organiser, the package travel contract is with Ambiente Tours GmbH and these conditions apply in full, including the insolvency protection in section 10.

2. Enquiry, offer, booking · Anfrage, Angebot, Buchung

An enquiry through the site or by email is not a booking. We reply with a written offer naming the ship, sailing date, cabin category, the fare per person, the service charge the line adds, what is and is not included, and the visa position for the ports on the route. The offer is valid for the period stated in it; cabin availability and fares are held only from the moment the line confirms a hold.

A booking comes into being when you accept the offer in writing (email suffices) and we send the booking confirmation. Please check the confirmation on receipt; names must match passports exactly, and a name correction later is charged by the line at its own rate.

3. Prices and what is included · Preise und Leistungen

Fares are quoted per person, in [EUR / the currency of the offer], for the cabin category named, two sharing unless stated. The confirmation lists what the fare includes for that ship; it differs by line. Not included unless the confirmation says so: flights, transfers, shore excursions, drinks packages, Wi‑Fi, speciality restaurants, spa, travel insurance, visas, and the line's daily service charge, whose amount we state in the offer. Port taxes and fees are included where the confirmation says they are.

Price changes after booking are possible only within § 651f BGB (fuel, taxes and fees, exchange rates), never later than 20 days before departure, and with a right to withdraw if the increase exceeds 8 %.

You may cancel at any time before departure. The cruise line's cancellation scale then applies to the cruise fare; it is printed in the offer and in the confirmation, and it is typically a percentage of the fare rising to 100 % in the last days before sailing. Where unavoidable and extraordinary circumstances at the destination significantly affect the cruise (§ 651h Abs. 3 BGB), you may cancel without fee; the line's or our assessment of the situation at the time of cancellation is what counts.

7. Passports, visas, health · Pass, Visum, Gesundheit

You are responsible for holding a valid passport and the visas the route requires. We state the visa position for every port in the offer, based on the passport you tell us you hold, and we say plainly where a port is Schengen, where a visa is easy but still needed, and where it is neither. That statement is advice, not a guarantee; entry rules change and are decided by the country concerned. Costs and consequences of a refused entry or a missing document are yours. Most lines require the passport to be valid six months beyond the end of the cruise.

For an arranged booking, AmbiMare is liable for its own agency duties (correct advice, correct transmission) and not for the performance of the cruise, which is the line's responsibility under its terms. Claims should be notified to us without undue delay so that we can help remedy the problem on board.
