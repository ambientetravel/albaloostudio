Travel law

Cruise24 books cruises in one of two roles, and every offer and confirmation says which applies. For a cruise on its own, we act as your travel agent; the cruise line is the organiser and protects your payments. When we combine a cruise with flights, hotels or transfers, Ambiente Tours GmbH is the organiser, protects your payments and sends the security certificate with the confirmation. The details are in our booking conditions.

Cruise24 is a brand of Ambiente Group. Ambiente Tours GmbH serves travellers in Germany, Austria and Switzerland; Ambiente Turizm Seyahat in Kuşadası, Türkiye, serves the Middle East, North Africa and Türkiye. The group's office in Italy is Boutique Mare in Rome. More about us.
