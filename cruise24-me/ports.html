Ports

Ports of Türkiye, Greece and Italy

Where our ships call most, and what each port is like to arrive in. The counts are from the sailings on this site today.

Türkiye

Türkiye

123 sailings call here. See them all.

Istanbul

Ships berth at Galataport in Karaköy, on the Bosphorus: Galata is a walk away and the old city a short tram ride.

84 of our sailings call here (Scenic, Seabourn, Silversea, Regent Seven Seas, Explora Journeys, Star Clippers, Sea Cloud, Ponant).

Kuşadası and Ephesus

The pier is in the middle of town and Ephesus is about twenty minutes by road. Our full port guide.

68 of our sailings call here (Ponant, Silversea, Regent Seven Seas, Explora Journeys, Seabourn, Star Clippers, Scenic, SeaDream, Sea Cloud).

Bodrum

The Castle of St Peter and the harbour front are the centre of town; the site of the Mausoleum is a short walk inland.

22 of our sailings call here (Ponant, Silversea, Explora Journeys, Star Clippers, SeaDream, Regent Seven Seas).

Marmaris

A deep, sheltered bay with the old town and castle behind the marina.

5 of our sailings call here (Regent Seven Seas, Explora Journeys).

Greece

Greece

238 sailings call here. See them all.

Athens (Piraeus)

Piraeus is Athens' port. The Acropolis is reached by road or metro; allow for traffic on a first or last day.

217 of our sailings call here (Ponant, Variety Cruises, SeaDream, Explora Journeys, Silversea, Seabourn, Star Clippers, Regent Seven Seas, Scenic, Sea Cloud).

Mykonos and Delos

Larger ships use the new port north of town, with a sea bus into Chora. Delos, the sanctuary island, is a short boat ride away.

103 of our sailings call here (Ponant, Variety Cruises, Silversea, Explora Journeys, Seabourn, SeaDream, Regent Seven Seas, Star Clippers).

Santorini

A tender port below Fira. The cable car or the steps climb the caldera cliff, and the cable-car queue is the thing to plan around.

78 of our sailings call here (Variety Cruises, Explora Journeys, Ponant, Silversea, Seabourn, Scenic, Star Clippers, Regent Seven Seas, SeaDream).

Corfu

The old town, a UNESCO World Heritage site of Venetian forts and arcades, is a short ride from the port.

53 of our sailings call here (Explora Journeys, Ponant, Seabourn, Regent Seven Seas, Scenic, SeaDream).

Nafplion

Greece's first capital. Ships anchor off the harbour; Mycenae and Epidaurus are both within reach for a half day.

52 of our sailings call here (Ponant, Variety Cruises, Silversea, Explora Journeys, Regent Seven Seas, Star Clippers, Seabourn, Sea Cloud).

Katakolon for Olympia

The port for Ancient Olympia, which lies inland by road.

None of our current sailings call here; ask us.

Patmos

Where tradition places St John's Book of Revelation. The Monastery of St John and the Cave of the Apocalypse are UNESCO-listed.

59 of our sailings call here (Ponant, Variety Cruises, Seabourn, Explora Journeys, Star Clippers, Silversea, SeaDream, Sea Cloud).

Rhodes

The medieval old town, a UNESCO site, begins at the harbour gates.

60 of our sailings call here (Silversea, Seabourn, Ponant, Regent Seven Seas, Explora Journeys, Star Clippers).

Italy and Malta

Italy and Malta

411 sailings call here. See them all.

Rome (Civitavecchia)

Civitavecchia is Rome's cruise port. For a day in the city the train from Civitavecchia station is the simplest option.

211 of our sailings call here (Silversea, SeaDream, Explora Journeys, Regent Seven Seas, Scenic, Star Clippers, Sea Cloud, Seabourn, Ponant).

Venice (Fusina)

Since 2021 large ships no longer sail the Giudecca Canal. Explora uses the Fusina terminal on the mainland, with a boat into the city.

99 of our sailings call here (Silversea, Explora Journeys, Regent Seven Seas, Seabourn).

Naples, Capri and the Amalfi Coast

Naples' terminal is in the city centre by the Castel Nuovo. Pompeii and Sorrento are day trips; smaller ships anchor off Amalfi, Sorrento and Capri.

134 of our sailings call here (Silversea, SeaDream, Explora Journeys, Regent Seven Seas, Star Clippers, Scenic, Seabourn, Ponant, Sea Cloud).

Sicily

Taormina sits above Giardini Naxos, where ships tender in. In Syracuse the island of Ortigia is walkable from the pier; Palermo's port is next to the old city.

143 of our sailings call here (Silversea, Sea Cloud, Ponant, Explora Journeys, SeaDream, Star Clippers, Regent Seven Seas, Seabourn, Scenic).

La Spezia for Cinque Terre, Florence and Pisa

The port for the Cinque Terre by local train, and for Florence and Pisa by road.

16 of our sailings call here (Explora Journeys, Regent Seven Seas).

Valletta, Malta

Ships berth below the Grand Harbour bastions; the Upper Barrakka lift takes you up into Valletta.

147 of our sailings call here (Silversea, Sea Cloud, Ponant, SeaDream, Star Clippers, Explora Journeys, Regent Seven Seas, Seabourn, Scenic, Variety Cruises).
