What happens next

Three steps, no hold music

1 · Options

Within one working day you get two or three sailings that fit, with the real cabin price for each category, the service charge, and the visa note.

2 · Hold

Pick one and we place a hold with the line. Most lines give a few days before a deposit is due; we tell you the exact date.

3 · Your file

The same planner stays on your file to the gangway: dining bookings, the thermal pass, the transfer, the question at 11pm the night before.
