Mediterranean & Western Europe

Genoa, Barcelona, Marseille, Naples, the Aegean from Athens. The most sailed sea in the world for a reason: a new city most mornings and a short flight home.

When
April–June, September–October
Lines
MSC, Explora Journeys, Celestyal, Variety
Visa
Schengen. Any Greek, Italian, Spanish or French port makes the whole cruise Schengen, even sailing from Istanbul. Türkiye stops are visa-free or e-visa for most passports.

Red Sea & Persian Gulf

Jeddah, Yanbu and Aqaba on the Red Sea; Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Doha and Bahrain in the Persian Gulf. Warm water when Europe is not.

When
November–March
Lines
AROYA, MSC, Explora Journeys
Visa
Easy visa, not no-visa. Saudi visa needed for the Red Sea loops; UAE, Qatar and Bahrain are e-visa or on arrival for many passports. Only AROYA's Egypt and Türkiye routes are truly visa-free. Tell us your passport and we check.
