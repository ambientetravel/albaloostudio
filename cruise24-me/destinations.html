Sailings by season

Sailings we recommend, season by season

Fares are live from the lines and quoted per person for the cheapest available cabin, two sharing. The line adds a daily service charge separately. What we add is the note on each port: how far the town is from the pier, whether a visa is needed, and whether the stop is worth a tour or a walk.

Worked example

Aegean islands from Athens, 7 nights

Celestyal Journey. Departs Piraeus most Saturdays, April to October. The Greek ports are Schengen, so the whole cruise needs a Schengen visa unless your passport is Schengen visa-exempt; the Kuşadası stop itself is visa-free or e-visa for most nationalities.

  • Day 1
    Athens (Piraeus)

    Embark from 13:00. Sail 19:00.

  • Day 2
    Thessaloniki

    Long day in port. Walk the waterfront, skip the bus tour.

  • Day 3
    Kuşadası, Türkiye

    Ephesus is 20 minutes away and worth it. Town is five minutes from the pier. Our port guide.

  • Day 4
    Rhodes

    Old town is at the pier. Lindos needs a car.

  • Day 5
    Heraklion, Crete

    Knossos in the morning, lunch in town.

  • Day 6
    Santorini

    Tender port. Go early or go late, never at 11:00.

  • Day 7
    Mykonos

    Overnight in port. The good restaurants are inland.

  • Day 8
    Athens

    Disembark by 09:00.
