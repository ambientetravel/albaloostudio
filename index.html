The Vision

Sound crafted from code, stories born from ancient fire

alBaloo Studio is a music and video studio where AI-generated artists come to life — not as imitations, but as entirely new creative beings. Each artist is built from scratch: their sound, their story, their visual identity, all born from code.

What connects them is mythology. From the endurance epics of Persia to the whispered poetry of Rumi, from Spartan battle cries to the silk roads of Istanbul — we bridge civilizations through rhythm and melody.

DAVAN

The Runner

دویدن Davidan = to run. DA1 = Day One. Every run is Day One.

Heavy percussion-driven electronic. Minimal lyrics, maximum beats. Male vocal war cry shouts as percussion — no full singing. A sonic warrior who turns ancient mythology into fuel for the modern endurance athlete.

ROXANA

The Poet

روشنک Roshanak = bright dawn. The Achaemenid princess who bridged empires.

Diaspora fusion — Persian pop meets Turkish makam meets modern electronic and dance rhythms. Breathy, sensual female vocal weaving between Persian, Turkish, and English. Sensual but artistic — the warmth of desire through music, not explicit.
