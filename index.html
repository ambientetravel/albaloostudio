The Vision

The Alchemy of alBaloo

In Farsi, Albaloo is the sour cherry — a brilliant, ruby-red fruit that masterfully balances the sweet nectar of life with a sharp, awakening bite. For millennia, it has been the defining flavor of the Persian palate. Whether woven into a magical tapestry of golden saffron and fragrant rice, or simmered into rich, timeless sweets, Albaloo is the taste of duality. It is the old and the new, the ancient and the modern, the bittersweet symphony of existence.

As a record label, Albaloo embodies this exact alchemy. We believe music, like our namesake, should encompass the full spectrum of the human experience. We are here to bring the ancient echoes of Oriental mythology and culture into the modern world — not just through instruments, but through the limitless possibilities of Artificial Intelligence.

By harmonizing ancestral wisdom with advanced AI coding, we are cultivating a new generation of digital artists. We create voices that tell thousand-year-old stories. We sing the ecstatic, soul-stirring poetry of Rumi and the lyrical romance of Hafiz. We pump endurance and cinematic power into your veins with the epic heroics of the Shahnameh.

Give your daily life a taste of Albaloo.

The Runner

دویدن Davidan = to run. DA1 = Day One. Every run is Day One.

The Poet

روشنک Roshanak = bright dawn. The Achaemenid princess who bridged empires.
